StreamDek Sans Rounded — licensing and provenance record
Review build 0.910, 11 October 2026

ORIGINAL PROJECT ASSETS

The StreamDek Sans Rounded outlines, generator, masters, feature definitions and
specimen layouts were newly generated for this StreamDek commission with AI
assistance. No pre-existing font outlines were imported, traced, renamed,
rescaled or modified to construct the StreamDek Sans Rounded font files.

The original generated assets are delivered to the requesting user for use,
modification, embedding and distribution as part of the StreamDek project,
subject to the terms governing that user's receipt of generated output.
This package imposes no additional third-party font licensing requirement on
those original outlines. It does not declare an open-source release, assign
third-party rights, or claim that AI-generated output necessarily qualifies
for exclusive copyright protection. The StreamDek name and logo remain
subject to their existing ownership; no trademark rights are granted here.

This provenance is evidence of an independent construction process, not a
global trademark search or legal certification of non-infringement. The
recipient should determine its final copyright notice and public licensing
policy before releasing the editable family to third parties. Nothing here
requires publishing proprietary StreamDek application source.

REFERENCE ASSETS — SEPARATE FROM THE FAMILY

references/GoogleSans-Regular.ttf is an unchanged Google Sans reference
downloaded from Google's font service. Its separate license is preserved as
references/GoogleSans-OFL.txt. It is used only for the comparison specimen;
it is not an input to the StreamDek Sans Rounded generator. Preserve its license when
redistributing the reference package. It may be omitted from an application
font distribution, which needs only the selected StreamDek Sans Rounded files.

BUILD TOOLS

fontTools, Brotli, skia-pathops, uharfbuzz, Pillow and Playwright are external
build/validation dependencies. Their packages are not included in the release
ZIP. See their own distributions for their licenses. The produced font outlines
are not borrowed from any fonts that those libraries may be able to read.
